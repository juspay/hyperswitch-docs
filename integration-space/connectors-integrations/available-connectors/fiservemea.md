# Fiserv IPG (EMEA)

Fiserv IPG (EMEA) processes credit and debit card payments through Hyperswitch. Its card routes support refunds and manual capture, while 3DS is not supported. The capability table lists the declared card networks, regions, and currencies.

### Webhooks

Fiserv IPG (EMEA) webhooks are not supported. The webhook methods return `WebhooksNotImplemented`; use payment sync and refund sync for status. See [`IncomingWebhook for Fiservemea`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/7a348f02a94edf7b2593e65ca6ffaf8d8f9ee6f8/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/fiservemea.rs#L773-L797), [`ConnectorIntegration<PSync>`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/7a348f02a94edf7b2593e65ca6ffaf8d8f9ee6f8/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/fiservemea.rs#L360-L435), and [`ConnectorIntegration<RSync>`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/7a348f02a94edf7b2593e65ca6ffaf8d8f9ee6f8/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/fiservemea.rs#L697-L771).
