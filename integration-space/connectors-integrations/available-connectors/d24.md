# D24

D24 routes card payments through a hosted redirect. It supports payments in Chile only, in CLP and USD. The redirect completes with automatic capture and supports refunds. Mandates are not available for this payment route.

### Authentication

Provide the **API Key**, **Read-only API Key**, and **API Signature** requested by the connector configuration. These labels map to the three accepted signature-key fields in [`D24AuthType::try_from()`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/d03f8547d6367fab56690e4f5043590be4935dcd/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/d24/transformers.rs#L74-L94) and are declared in the [`d24.connector_auth.SignatureKey` configuration](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/d03f8547d6367fab56690e4f5043590be4935dcd/crates/connector_configs/toml/sandbox.toml#L9155-L9158).

### Before you start

For D24 account setup or enablement in Hyperswitch, reach out to the Hyperswitch team on Slack or through [Juspay's contact form](https://juspay.io/contact?country=US). D24 is not available in the Hyperswitch control center.

### Webhooks

D24 webhooks are not currently supported. Use Hyperswitch's Unified Connector Service (UCS) to sync payment and refund status. D24's direct connector sync methods are not used. See [`IncomingWebhook for D24`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/d03f8547d6367fab56690e4f5043590be4935dcd/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/d24.rs#L348-L371), the [`UCS routing configuration`](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/d03f8547d6367fab56690e4f5043590be4935dcd/config/development.toml#L1684), and [D24 refund routing](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch/blob/d03f8547d6367fab56690e4f5043590be4935dcd/crates/hyperswitch_connectors/src/connectors/d24.rs#L339-L345).
