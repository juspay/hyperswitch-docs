# Additional Offer Capabilities

Create more reasons for customers to buy with personalized promotions, product discounts, and rewards. Explore these additional offer capabilities with Hyperswitch, available on request.

| Capability                    | What you can do                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product-based offers          | Create promotions for specific products or cart contents, with conditions based on product quantity or price.                |
| Combining multiple offers     | Let customers benefit from compatible promotions on the same payment, such as a product discount combined with a card offer. |
| Coupons and private offers    | Let customers enter coupon codes, or share unique codes with selected customers for targeted campaigns.                      |
| Customer-based offers         | Target customer groups using customer IDs, email addresses, or phone numbers.                                                |
| Custom eligibility rules      | Use merchant-defined attributes, such as membership tier or campaign identifier, to determine offer eligibility.             |
| Scheduled promotions          | Run offers during selected hours, days, or dates—for example, a weekend sale.                                                |
| Offers across payment methods | Extend promotions to eligible wallets, and bank payment methods.                                                             |

Contact your Hyperswitch point of contact to enable the capabilities that fit your campaigns and checkout experience.
